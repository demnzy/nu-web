Everything in One App

Everything You Need to Study

Take courses, revise your notes, practice past questions, and chat with coursemates.

1. Interactive Courses & Lessons

Learn at your own pace with over 30 courses. Lessons include videos, notes, quizzes, and downloads.

  • In-course AI Assistant: Ask questions while you watch or read
  • Discussion Forums: Ask questions under any lesson slide and learn with peers
  • Verifiable Certificates: Earn certificates that anyone can verify with a QR code

2. Flashcards from Your Notes

Upload your lecture handouts or photograph your handwritten notes. Nu Age turns them into flashcards.

  • Quick Flashcards: Swipe through short cards to remember key definitions and formulas
  • Sorted by Course: Group flashcards by course code, topic, or semester
  • ₦0 Data While Revising: Study anywhere without internet

3. Timed Practice Exams

Practice past questions and mock tests against the clock before your real exam.

  • Countdown Timer: Practice managing your time
  • Skip & Flag: Move between questions and flag hard ones to revisit
  • Score Review: See your score and step-by-step answers right away

4. Quick Quizzes

Short on time? Take a 5-minute quiz after a handout or lecture to test what you learned.

  • 5-Minute Quizzes: Short tests that help you remember what you studied
  • Check Your Understanding: See if you really understood the topic
  • Find Weak Spots: Shows which topics to review again

5. In-App Nu Chat & Study Groups

Stop losing course discussions in busy social media groups. Chat with your coursemates inside Nu Age.

  • Direct Messaging: Chat 1-on-1 with coursemates and exchange study tips
  • Group Chats: Study together in course group chats
  • Lesson Discussions: Get answers from tutors and classmates
See Chat Interface →

6. Campus Networking & Connect

Find students in your field at universities across Nigeria and work on projects together.

  • Discover Students: Meet students in your department, school, or state
  • Study Partnerships: Find study partners and prepare for exams together
  • Resource Sharing: Share notes, exam tips, and study resources
