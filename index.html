Everything in One App

More than Just a Study App. A Complete Student Hub.

Everything you need for your university journey: take courses, revise your personal handouts, practice past questions with real timers, and chat with coursemates.

1. Interactive Courses & Lessons

Learn at your own pace with over 30 structured courses. Lessons feature video lectures, clean reading notes, interactive quizzes, and downloadable resources.

  • In-course AI Assistant: Ask questions right beside your video or handout when you get stuck
  • Discussion Forums: Ask questions under any lesson slide and learn with peers
  • Verifiable Certificates: Earn digital completion certificates with instant QR verification

2. Smart Revision Flashcards

Upload your personal university lecture handouts, syllabus slides, or snap photos of handwritten notes. Nu Age automatically turns them into interactive revision flashcards.

  • Active Recall Cards: Swipe through bite-sized cards that help you remember key definitions and formulas
  • Organized by Subject: Keep revision decks organized by course code, topic, or semester
  • ₦0 Data While Revising: Study on the bus, in class, or at home with zero internet connection

3. Realistic Timed Exam Simulator

Build exam-room speed and endurance before your actual test day. Practice official university past questions and mock tests under realistic countdown pressure.

  • Real Countdown Timers: Train yourself to manage time effectively under realistic conditions
  • Question Navigation & Flagging: Jump freely between questions and flag tough problems to review later
  • Detailed Score Reviews: Get immediate diagnostic breakdowns with step-by-step explanations

4. Quick Diagnostic Quizzes

Short on time? Take 5-minute diagnostic checks right after reading a handout or watching a lecture to test what you just learned.

  • 5-Minute Micro Checks: Rapid tests designed to reinforce memory right after study sessions
  • Instant Knowledge Verification: Prove to yourself that you actually understood the concept
  • Weak Spot Detection: Immediately highlights which topics need a quick second review

5. In-App Nu Chat & Study Groups

Stop losing course discussions in noisy social media groups. Nu Age gives you direct, focused messaging built right into your learning environment.

  • Direct Messaging: Chat 1-on-1 with coursemates and exchange study tips
  • Cohort & Group Chats: Study together in focused subject channels
  • Lesson Discussions: Get answers from tutors and colleagues on difficult concepts

6. Campus Networking & Connect

Find other students studying your discipline across universities in Nigeria. Build academic connections and collaborate on projects.

  • Discover Students: Connect with peers in your department, school, or state
  • Study Partnerships: Form study partnerships and prepare for exams together
  • Resource Sharing: Share curated notes, exam tips, and learning resources
