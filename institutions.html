Built for Campuses

Built for How African Campuses Work

Platforms that need constant internet fail when campus Wi-Fi drops. Nu Age is designed for weak connections and many students online at once.

Exam Security

Secure Computer-Based Exams

Run important tests with confidence. Includes browser lockdown, focus monitoring, tab-switch alerts, randomized questions, and signed submissions.

  • Automatic incident logs and warning limits that end the exam
  • Random question and answer order to stop students copying
  • Offline completion receipts that stay valid when the network is down
Local Network

Campus Network Servers

Install small servers in computer labs, libraries, or lecture halls. Students download course videos and take exams over the campus network without using outside internet.

  • Exams keep running even if the university's internet line is cut
  • Fast local video for 1,000+ students at once
  • Results sync automatically to the university database
Certificates

Certificates That Can't Be Faked

Stop fake credentials. Every completed course, module, and exam produces a digital certificate with a QR code that shows if it has been altered.

  • Employers and academic boards scan the QR code to check it is real
  • Includes the university seal and the Dean's digital signature
  • Download as PDF or share a link on LinkedIn or your CV
Governance

Departments & Faculties

Set roles for Dean, Head of Department, Lecturer, Teaching Assistant, and Student. Manage who can do what, department syllabi, and student lists.

  • Departments can approve syllabi and review courses
  • Assign several lecturers to a course with their own grading and editing rights
  • Student lists linked to matriculation numbers
Quality & Reporting

Reports for Accreditation

Track how many students stay, continuous assessment progress, and who is at risk of dropping out across whole faculties. Download reports.

  • Grades and continuous assessment results across a faculty
  • Spot at-risk students from quiz activity and attendance
  • One-click export for NUC and professional accreditation panels
We Move Your Content

We Digitize Your Courses

Our engineers work with your academic board to turn old course outlines, question banks, and slides into digital courses.

  • Fast conversion of paper past questions into digital exam question banks
  • Syllabus and lesson planning by course designers
  • Campus setup and training for your IT staff
