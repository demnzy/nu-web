Enterprise Architecture

Built to Withstand African Campus Realities

Western cloud-only platforms crash when campus Wi-Fi drops. Nu Age was engineered from the ground up for low-bandwidth, high-concurrency campus environments.

Examination Security

High-Integrity CBT Examination Runner

Conduct high-stakes tests with confidence. Features browser lockdown, focus monitoring, tab switch alerts, randomized question banks, and cryptographically signed submission tokens.

  • Automated violation logging and maximum infraction limits with auto-termination
  • Randomized question order and option shuffling to prevent exam room collusion
  • Cryptographic offline completion receipts that validate even during network cuts
Local Infrastructure

Local Campus Edge LAN Nodes

Deploy lightweight edge servers directly in campus computer labs, libraries, or lecture halls. Students sync heavy course videos and take examinations over local intranet without consuming external internet bandwidth.

  • Zero-WAN exam running: tests continue smoothly even if the university's ISP fiber cuts
  • High-speed local video distribution with zero buffering for 1,000+ simultaneous students
  • Automatic background ledger synchronization back to the central university database
Credential Integrity

Tamper-Proof Verifiable Certificates

Eliminate academic credential fraud. Every completed course, professional module, and examination generates a tamper-evident digital certificate with an instant QR code.

  • Instant QR verification: employers and academic boards scan to verify authenticity
  • Branded with university seal, Dean's digital signature, and cryptographic hash
  • Downloadable PDF and shareable digital credential link for LinkedIn and resumes
Governance

Department & Faculty Hierarchy

Hierarchical role-based access control (Dean, Head of Department, Lecturer, Teaching Assistant, Student). Manage course permissions, department syllabi, and student enrollment rosters seamlessly.

  • Department-level syllabus approval and curriculum oversight workflows
  • Multi-lecturer assignment per course with tailored grading and authoring permissions
  • Unified enrollment roster sync with university matriculation numbers
Compliance & Quality

Accreditation-Ready Audit Analytics

Track student retention, continuous assessment milestones, and drop-off risks across entire university faculties with downloadable audit reports.
